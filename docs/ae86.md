# 1983 Toyota Sprinter Trueno GT-APEX

Tofu Touge’s second car follows the Japanese-market three-door GT-APEX that Toyota introduced on 12 May 1983. The short nose, retractable headlamps, sloping glass liftback and narrow rear pillars come from the early car. Toyota’s 2014 Fujiwara tofu-shop replica provided the reference for the two-tone body, signwritten right-hand door, yellow fog lamps, rear badges and burgundy cabin. ## In the game

The model is built in metres around those axle and bumper positions. The game car wears period-inspired graphite eight-spokes with 185/60R14 tyres. The Japan Automobile Federation lists both this size and 185/70HR13 among the AE86’s catalogue fitments. The wheel castings and brake hardware are modelled separately, so the calipers stay with the hub while the discs turn with the wheels. [JAF catalogue tyre and weight register](https://motorsports.jaf.or.jp/-/media/1/3375/3379/3401/0970_prep_201712.pdf)

The simulation adds a 65 kg driver. The torque curve between Toyota’s published peaks, tyre grip, rolling radius under load, 850 rpm idle and 7,600 rpm limiter are game tuning. Original SuperCollider recordings blend the four-cylinder exhaust, engine braking and opening induction with revs and throttle, following the character of Toyota’s two-stage T-VIS intake.
